Aura for Frigate

Support

Aura is an independent camera viewer for Frigate NVR on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Connect a server

  1. Open Settings → Server and enter a Frigate address and port your device can reach.
  2. Add a username and password if your Frigate server requires them.
  3. For live video, set the scheme and port for your go2rtc endpoint.

If you use a local address, connect to the same network as your Frigate server. Allow local network access when your device asks.

Get help

Email aura@fardavide.dev. Include your Aura version, device, OS version, Frigate version, and the steps that led to the problem. Leave out passwords, private server addresses, and camera footage.
