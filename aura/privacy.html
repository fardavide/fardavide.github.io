Aura for Frigate

Privacy Policy

Aura connects to the Frigate server you choose. During normal use, the developer does not receive your camera footage, server credentials, or app usage data. Aura has no developer-hosted account or footage library and uses no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Connecting to your server

Aura sends requests to your configured Frigate and go2rtc addresses to show your cameras, recordings, and events. If you enter credentials, Aura uses them to authenticate with your Frigate endpoint. Your server and any proxy you configure may log those requests. Choose HTTPS in Aura when your endpoint supports it to protect requests in transit.

What stays on your device

Aura stores connection details, your username if provided, and app preferences on your device. Your server password is stored in the system Keychain. Aura may hold media temporarily during playback or download. When you save or share a clip, the destination you select receives that copy.

Removing your data

You can edit connection details and clear your username and password in Settings. Use Frigate to manage recordings, detections, and exports stored on your server. Manage clips you have saved or shared in the app or folder where you saved them.

Contact

When you email aura@fardavide.dev, the developer receives your email address and the information you include. The developer uses it to respond to your request and keeps it as needed to handle the request and any follow-up. You can use the same address for privacy questions and to ask for deletion of information you sent to support.
